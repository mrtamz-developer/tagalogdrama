# Privacy release check

Before production launch, verify:

- [ ] Privacy Policy accurately describes data collected and used.
- [ ] Terms of Service are final and accessible.
- [ ] Account deletion/data deletion process is defined where accounts exist.
- [ ] Analytics are disabled until configured with appropriate disclosures/consent requirements.
- [ ] Payment data is handled by the payment provider/backend rather than stored in the public frontend.
- [ ] Logs do not unnecessarily expose passwords, tokens, payment details, or sensitive user data.
- [ ] Third-party SDKs and data-sharing behavior have been reviewed.
- [ ] Production contact/support information is current.

Keep private user data and confidential legal documents out of this public repository.

# Launch blockers

The following items must remain unresolved before a production launch:

- Unverified or unlicensed media
- Exposed secrets or privileged credentials
- Client-only enforcement of payments or premium access
- Unprotected production video URLs
- Unverified payment/ad webhooks
- Missing required privacy or terms disclosures
- Broken authentication or account recovery
- Critical mobile/PWA failures
- Failed security testing

A cosmetic issue may be deferred; any item above is a release blocker.

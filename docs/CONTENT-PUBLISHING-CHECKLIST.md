# Content publishing checklist

Before publishing a title or episode:

- [ ] Rights holder and license/permission reference are recorded.
- [ ] Territory and license dates are confirmed.
- [ ] Streaming and monetization rights are confirmed.
- [ ] Video file has been reviewed for quality and playback compatibility.
- [ ] Thumbnail/artwork rights are verified.
- [ ] Title, description, episode number, duration, and age/content rating are reviewed.
- [ ] No private source URLs, credentials, or confidential documents are exposed.
- [ ] Asset is marked `APPROVED` in the content-rights register.
- [ ] Final QA passes on mobile and desktop.

Never publish an asset marked `PENDING`, `UNKNOWN`, expired, or `DO NOT PUBLISH`.
